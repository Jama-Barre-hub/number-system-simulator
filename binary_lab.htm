What this simulator demonstrates

BinaryLab shows how the binary number system (base 2) relates to decimal, octal and hexadecimal, and how binary arithmetic works — including the fractional part after the binary point.

Convert module

  • You enter a binary number. Pressing Next walks through the conversion one system at a time: first Decimal, then Octal, then Hexadecimal.
  • Decimal uses positional weights (powers of 2). Octal groups bits in 3s. Hex groups bits in 4s.

Add & Subtract module

  • Binary addition and subtraction, column by column, with carries / borrows and a decimal check at the end.
